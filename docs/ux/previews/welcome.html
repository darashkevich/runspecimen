You approve what may run.
Then you get a receipt you can keep.

When an assistant or a script is about to do something that matters, a green “done” is not enough. You review a short plan, you type APPROVE yourself, and you get a receipt of what actually ran.

1
Review the plan
See what may run, which files count, and what must be true afterward.
2
You type APPROVE
Assistants cannot do this for you. This app will not type it either.
3
Keep the receipt
Later you can tell, in one glance, whether anything changed.
What this does not do

It does not lock the rest of your Mac. It does not prove your work is true. It does not schedule jobs. The receipt is a checkable history, not a bank-style signature.

Stays on this Mac. No account. No telemetry.

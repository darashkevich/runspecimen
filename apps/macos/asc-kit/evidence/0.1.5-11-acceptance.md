Installer fingerprint begins `DF 49 69 B0`. `pkgutil` also prints the generic status “developer certificate issued by Apple (Development)”; the leaf certificate is the Mac App Store installer identity, not Developer ID.
- Export log: `Local-only export (destination=export)` and `EXPORT SUCCEEDED`. No upload.

- `verify_mas_runtime.py` on the archive app and on the extracted payload: 48 Mach-O files, 0 violations. Privacy manifest, `AppIcon.icns`, and `ReviewerDemo/contract.json` are in the payload.
- `Scripts/test_store_export_gate.sh` printed `STORE EXPORT GATE TESTS OK` before this export (tamper-after-signing, ad-hoc, Developer ID, mismatched archive app, and test-fixture identities refused).

Onboarding clips

The core flow, in a few seconds each.

RunSpecimen puts a human approval gate and a tamper-evident receipt around a bounded local run. These GIFs replay real CLI text from runspecimen init-demo: review the contract, type APPROVE, run once, then verify the certificate.

Additive marketing assets. They do not approve or execute anything in your browser. Rebuild with python3 web/onboarding/build_gifs.py.
